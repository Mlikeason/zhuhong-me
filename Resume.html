For 23 years I’ve worked at the seam where people, software, and hardware meet — operating systems, devices, brands, and the everyday products that turn technology into something people actually use.
What pulls my attention now is how AI is redrawing that seam. Three questions in particular:
— How AI reshapes the user experience itself: discovery, guidance, the small moments that make a product feel intelligent.
— What happens when AI meets hardware, when an experience spans screen, device, and physical space instead of living inside an app.
— How AI agents can reimagine the way creative teams are structured and run — augmenting specialists rather than replacing them.
That curiosity sits on top of a long path: shaping Microsoft’s Metro design language across Windows, leading international design for Xiaomi’s MIUI, consulting for brands including Tesla, and now leading design at Lazada — named a Top 10 Global Innovation Center by IxDC × WDO in 2024.
I care most about the environment around the work — teams that trust each other do their most original thinking — and I believe the next decade of design will come down to how well we pair human craft with AI.
